# What is Shell Eco-marathon (for CUERT)?

## Overview
Shell Eco-marathon is a global competition that challenges student teams to design, build, and drive ultra energy-efficient vehicles. It is one of the most prestigious engineering competitions worldwide, focusing on innovation, sustainability, and real-world mobility solutions.

For the Cairo University Eco Racing Team (CUERT), Shell Eco-marathon represents the core platform where engineering knowledge meets practical application.

---

## CUERT’s Participation

CUERT represents Egypt in Shell Eco-marathon competitions, competing against top universities from around the world. The team participates in multiple categories, including:

- Mileage Challenge (fuel efficiency)
- Autonomous Driving Competition
- Prototype and Urban Concept vehicle classes
- ⁠Communications spirit award 
- ⁠Telemetry award

The goal is to achieve the highest possible energy efficiency, measured in:
- km/L (for gasoline vehicles)
- km/kWh (for electric vehicles)

According to the team’s performance targets:
- Over **200 km/L** for gasoline vehicles  
- Over **500 km/kWh** for electric vehicles 
---

## Why It Matters to CUERT

Shell Eco-marathon is not just a competition it is the main driver of CUERT’s technical development. It allows the team to:

- Apply theoretical engineering knowledge in real-world systems  
- Develop innovative, eco-friendly vehicle technologies  
- Compete on an international level  
- Represent Egypt globally  

It also pushes the team to work on advanced areas such as:
- Energy optimization  
- Lightweight materials (e.g., carbon fiber chassis)  
- Autonomous navigation systems  

---

## Impact on the Team

Participation in Shell Eco-marathon has helped CUERT achieve:

- Record-breaking efficiency results  
- Global rankings and awards  
- Recognition in international and regional competitions  
- Development of both gasoline and electric prototype vehicles
